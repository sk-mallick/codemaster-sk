FAQ

Quick answers.

Working together, simply.

What kind of projects do you work on?

I work on responsive business websites, portfolios, education platforms, dashboards, student portals, management tools, and selected automation workflows.

Can you build a website from scratch?

Yes. I can help shape the structure, implement the UI, add suitable functionality, and prepare the website for deployment.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. I can review its layout, responsiveness, accessibility, performance, and interaction patterns, then prioritize practical improvements.

What technologies do you use?

My frontend work focuses on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with supporting backend and database tools when needed.

How do we get started?

Send a short description of your idea, your goals, and any deadline or reference. I'll review the scope and discuss a realistic next step.
